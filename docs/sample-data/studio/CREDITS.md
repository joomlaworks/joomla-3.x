# Studio sample data: image credits

The images of the "Studio" sample data set (installed with Joomla 3.x UTD, or from the Sample Data module), bundled in
`images/sampledata/studio/`. All of them are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, change and distribute, without attribution. They're
credited anyway. They were published on Unsplash while it used CC0, and are kept on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).
Each one was cropped to 16:9 and saved as WebP at 1920 × 1080 (`<name>.webp`) and 960 × 540 (`<name>-960.webp`, for small screens).
The set's other images are the News set's (see `../news/CREDITS.md`).
