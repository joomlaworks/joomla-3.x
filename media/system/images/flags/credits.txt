Language flags (36x24 pixels, shown at 18x12), made for Joomla 3.x UTD from:

- Country flags: the PNG renders of svg-country-flags 1.2.10 by Hampus Nilsson
  (https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags), public domain.
- The Arab League (ar, ar_aa), Catalonia (ca, ca_es), the Basque Country (eu_es), Galicia (gl, gl_es) and the East
  African Community (sw, sw_ke): flag-icons 7.6.0 (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence below.
- Esperanto (eo, eo_xx), Kurdistan (ku, cbk_iq), Quebec (fr_ca) and the Assyrian flag (sy, sy_iq): Wikimedia Commons
  (Flag of Esperanto.svg, Flag of Kurdistan.svg, Flag of Quebec.svg, Flag of Assyria.svg), public domain.
- Brittany (br, br_fr) and the script names (si, si_lk, ta, ta_in, ug_cn, in Noto Sans): drawn for Joomla 3.x UTD,
  GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
